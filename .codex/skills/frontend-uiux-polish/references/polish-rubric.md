# Polish Rubric

Use this rubric when the prompt is asking for professional UI or UX polish on a FitTrack surface.

## Layout

- spacing rhythm is consistent within and between sections
- the page has a clear scan path
- primary actions stand out without overwhelming the page
- cards, sections, and modal bodies do not feel cramped or overpadded

## Hierarchy

- headings, metadata, actions, and supporting text have clear visual weight
- related controls are grouped together
- destructive or secondary actions are not mistaken for the primary path

## Theme coherence

- colors feel like the current app, not a pasted-in design experiment
- accents are intentional and repeatable
- contrast and readability stay solid across cards, tables, filters, and modals

## Modal quality

- title and purpose are obvious
- the body has enough breathing room
- close, cancel, and submit actions are readable and predictable
- validation, success, and error feedback are visible

## Surface consistency

- reusable sections feel like part of one product family
- stale branding, awkward labels, and visible encoding glitches are fixed or classified
- the result keeps FitTrack's special visual character instead of flattening it into a generic dashboard
