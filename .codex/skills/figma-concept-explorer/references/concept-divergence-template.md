# Concept Divergence Template

Use this packet for every premium Figma concept phase.

## Required outputs

- `concept_a`
- `concept_b`
- `concept_c`
- `concept_divergence_matrix`
- `selected_concept`
- `selected_concept_rationale`
- `rejected_concept_reasons`
- `concept_to_reference_mapping`

## Divergence matrix axes

Each concept must differ on at least four of:

- `shell_silhouette`
- `command_surface_form`
- `material_language`
- `hierarchy_emphasis`
- `contrast_profile`
- `density_posture`

## Automatic fails

- three concepts share the same shell silhouette
- concepts differ mostly by labels, copy, or color accents
- the chosen concept can still be described as the anti-reference with nicer spacing
