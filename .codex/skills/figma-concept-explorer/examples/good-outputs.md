# Good Output Patterns

## Strong concept phase

- concept A, B, and C are visibly different at first glance
- the divergence matrix names at least four different axes per concept
- the chosen concept is justified by distinctiveness and operator fit, not by convenience
- rejected concepts are rejected for real reasons such as:
  - too close to anti-reference silhouette
  - same shell with different copy
  - strong mood but weak workflow fit
- the chosen concept can be handed directly to scaffold authoring without reopening direction

## Good signal

If a reviewer can tell which concept was selected and why from screenshots alone, the concept phase is doing its job.
