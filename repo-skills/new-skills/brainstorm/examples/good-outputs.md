# Good Outputs

## Example: pages feel technically done but dead

- Goal: make FitTrack pages feel professional and rewarding instead of static and flat.
- Hidden holes:
  - no distinction between structural UX problems and pure polish
  - modals, states, and interaction feedback may still be weak
  - some pages may be bloated enough that polish alone will not save them
- Recommended MVP:
  - enforce UI excellence on one benchmark page
  - fix hierarchy, adaptive layout, modal quality, async states, and interaction feedback
  - reserve celebration effects for achievement or streak moments only
- Risks to avoid:
  - random animation spam
  - redesigning the whole app before proving one page can be elevated well

## Example: new macro tracker idea

- Goal: add macro tracking without turning the product into a random nutrition app clone.
- Constraints:
  - keep first scope manageable
  - avoid coupling too many gamification layers immediately
- Recommended MVP:
  - daily macro targets
  - simple meal logging and progress feedback
  - one consistency-oriented reward loop later
